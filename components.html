Nav bar

Three separate shapes: an emerald logo tile that links home, a forest-green pill of icon links (work, playground, about, cv), and a forest-green email tile. On mobile it shrinks to the logo tile and a menu tile.

      <span class="sds-nav-icon"><img src="imgs/icons/briefcase.svg" alt=""></span>work</a></li>
    <!-- playground · about · cv -->
  </ul>
  <a class="sds-nav-email" href="mailto:…"><img src="imgs/icons/mail.svg" alt="Email"></a>
</nav>

Accessibility

  • 1.4.3Light Gray on Forest Green is 8.81:1 for the link labels.
  • 1.1.1The logo and email tiles have no visible text, so their images need alt text ("home", "Email"). Link icons sit next to text, so they use alt="".
  • 4.1.2The mobile menu tile is a <button> with aria-expanded and aria-controls. The links it reveals are not drawn in Figma, so they are a code-only addition.
